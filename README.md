## 🩺 System Capabilities & Clinical Engines

### 1. Physician & Doctor Tele-Consultation Portal (`/dashboard/doctor`)
* **Integrated WebRTC Video/Audio Room:** Secure, peer-to-peer real-time remote consultation between licensed physicians and patients.
* **Structured Electronic Medical Records (EMR):** Rapid clinical documentation engine covering **Symptoms**, **Physical Examination**, **Clinical Diagnosis**, and **Prescription Treatment Plans**.
* **Physician On-Duty Switch:** Instant operational duty tracking linked with clinical scheduling and appointment queues.

### 2. Emergency Operations & Lab Reception (`/emergency` & `/dashboard/lab-reception`)
* Fast-track triage for critical patient intake.
* Diagnostic specimen chain-of-custody tracking with instant electronic report dispatch.

### 3. Patient & Visitor Multi-Country Portal
* Multi-region SMS/OTP verification (🇦🇪 UAE +971, 🇧🇩 BD +880, 🇬🇧 UK, 🇺🇸 US) generating temporary digital clinical access passes.

### 4. Bed Census & Clinical PMS
* Dynamic ward, cabin, and ICU bed allocation matrix.
* Continuous patient folio billing, nursing station shifts, and surgical equipment charges.
